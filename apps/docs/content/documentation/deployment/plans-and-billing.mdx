Durabull Cloud subscriptions are billed **per organization**, not per person and not per job. The
desktop app and self-hosted installations under Apache-2.0 include every local feature without a
subscription and never contact Stripe.

Prices are in US dollars before applicable taxes. There are no fees per job, retry, or MCP call.
Individual job retries, inspection, and email alerts are available on every plan, and security
controls (encryption, tenant isolation, redaction) are never premium-only.

Enterprise is sold by agreement on shared Durabull Cloud. SAML SSO, SCIM, dedicated deployment, and
contractual SLAs are not part of any plan today.

## What counts

- **Connections**: each enabled connection to a Redis logical database counts once, however many
  queues it holds. A disconnected but enabled connection still counts. Disable a connection in
  **Settings → Connections** to stop its polling, alert evaluation, and operational access; disabled
  connections do not count. Different logical databases (or duplicate saved records) count separately.
- **Members**: the owner and active members count. Pending invitations reserve a seat until they are
  accepted, revoked, or expire. A person in two organizations uses a seat in each.
- **Alert rules and destinations**: enabled rules and saved destinations count. Disabling an extra rule
  keeps its configuration.
- **Service accounts**: enabled service accounts count against the plan's automation limit. They are
  separate from members and cannot sign in interactively.

## Buying, trials, and changes

Owners manage billing in **Settings → Billing**. Payment details, invoices, receipts, tax IDs, plan
changes, and cancellation use Stripe-hosted pages; Durabull never sees card numbers. Other members see
the plan and usage and can ask the owner to upgrade.

- **Trial**: one 14-day Business trial per eligible organization, started from Settings → Billing,
  with no payment method required. Without a payment method the organization returns to Free when
  the trial ends.
- **Confirmation**: returning from checkout does not change access by itself. The plan updates once
  Stripe confirms payment, usually within seconds.
- **Upgrades** take effect after payment is confirmed. Until then the previous plan stays active.
- **Plan changes** are immediate with proration calculated by Stripe. Positive amounts are invoiced
  immediately; unused amounts from a downgrade normally become account credit rather than a card
  refund. Contact support about refunding unused credit or billing mistakes.
- **Cancellation** takes effect at the end of the paid period.
- **Annual plans** are prepaid: Team $90/year (equivalent to $7.50/month) and Business $290/year
  (about $24.17/month).

## Failed payments

If a renewal fails, a previously paid plan keeps working for **seven days** while you update the
payment method from Manage billing. After the grace period the organization uses Free limits. A new
subscription whose first payment fails does not unlock paid features.

## Over-limit resources after a downgrade

Billing never pauses your queues, deletes jobs, purges Redis, or changes schedules.

- New resources cannot be created above the new limits.
- Existing resources above the limits stay enabled for a **seven-day adjustment window**. The window is
  not extended by repeated upgrade and downgrade cycles, and there is no second window after a failed
  payment's grace period.
- In Settings → Billing the owner chooses which connections, members, rules, destinations, and service
  accounts stay enabled. Without a choice, Durabull keeps the owner and the oldest resources and pauses
  the newest extras.
- Paused resources keep their configuration and are restored automatically after an upgrade. Paused
  members cannot use existing sessions or tokens for that organization, paused connections stop
  polling, and webhook or Linear deliveries stop when the plan no longer includes them.

## History retention

Plan history windows apply to the Redis health samples Durabull stores, not to job payloads or logs,
which follow your own Redis and BullMQ retention. After a downgrade the visible window shortens
immediately; samples already collected are kept for a seven-day recovery period before being pruned
to the new window. Upgrading cannot recreate samples that were already pruned.
